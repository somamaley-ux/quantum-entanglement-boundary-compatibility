\begin{abstract}
This manuscript proves a kernel-role determinacy theorem for no-signaling and Bell witness fixation. It does not modify Hilbert-space quantum mechanics, derive the Born rule, introduce hidden variables, add collapse dynamics, or choose a constructive mechanism generating Bell correlations. Its claim is stronger and more precise: once Bell-type operational data are used as a non-degenerate reusable witness object, their determinacy is governed by the kernel roles of admissibility, standing, reference, and irreversibility. Quantum theory and experiment supply physical Bell correlation occurrence; the present theorem identifies the admissible same-domain normal form of Bell-witness objecthood and the terminal no-signaling endpoint---the exclusion of same-domain signal, transport, marginal-modulation, and distant-repair typings---forced by that normal form.

The flagship result is no-signaling as a same-domain determinacy theorem. Given Bell-violating witness content treated as a non-degenerate Bell object, the kernel roles exclude every same-domain typing of the correlations as superluminal signal, metric transport, distant standing repair, hidden local authorizer, delayed calibration repair, or extra discriminator. On the fixed Bell scope, setting, outcome, detector, calibration, readout, and coincidence interfaces enter only through declared stable boundary traces. An entangled preparation is therefore not typed as two independently complete local standing states later connected by a mechanism, nor as a distribution over pre-assigned local values, nor as a superluminal influence. It is typed as a joint standing class carrying tensor-level compatibility constraints, sampled locally without marginal-modulation authority. The family of outcome realizations carries Bell correlation structure because local measurements realize compatible faces of one AMetrically co-standing joint class; the correlation is not produced by communication, metric transport, or distant-state repair.

The result is eliminative in proof method, ontic in target, and constraint-theoretic in force. The numerical no-signaling equations are part of the fixed Bell witness set; the AASC endpoint is stronger than restating them. It proves that no admissible same-domain construction can reinterpret that witness as signal, transport, distant repair, hidden local value-completion, non-trace detector selection, global-apparatus authority, or extra-discriminator work without changing scope or importing hidden load. Once those routes are closed, the surviving same-domain determinacy normal form is boundary-level compatibility: tensor-level compatibility carried by a joint standing class and sampled locally through boundary-trace-fixed realizations. This is the Bell-witness companion to the measurement-record determinacy theorem: the record paper proves that non-degenerate evidential record objecthood requires admissible record-role fixation; the present paper proves that non-degenerate Bell-witness objecthood requires boundary-level compatibility and no-signaling under the kernel roles.

\section{Introduction}
Quantum entanglement sits at the intersection of two pressures that standard physics language often treats as if they required a mechanism. On one side, the EPR argument sharpened the question whether quantum mechanics is complete by focusing on joint systems whose measurement statistics exhibit perfect correlations across incompatible bases \cite{Einstein1935}. On the other side, Bell's theorem and the CHSH formulation show that no Bell-local hidden-variable factorization reproduces the full quantum correlation structure of certain entangled states \cite{Bell1964,CHSH1969,Brunner2014}. Yet the operational formalism satisfies no-signaling constraints: the local marginal distribution on one wing does not depend on which measurement is chosen on the other \cite{Brunner2014}. The standard result is a structural split. The correlations exceed Bell-local realism, but they are not usable as superluminal communication. The flagship endpoint of the present theorem is the AASC determination of that split: Bell violation rules out independently sufficient local standing, while the kernel roles rule out same-domain signal, transport, and distant-repair typings.

The present paper takes that split as the point at which Bell-witness determinacy becomes visible. It does not choose among physical mechanisms that may generate correlations. It identifies the determinacy conditions any such account must satisfy when the Bell table is reused as the same witness across settings, relabelings, experimental records, and later classification. The distinction matters. A table of operational probabilities may physically occur as data. But a Bell witness is not merely a numerical trace. It is a standing object of comparison and reuse. If it is non-degenerate in that role, it must preserve reference, standing, admissibility, and irreversibility.

The usual literature presents the Bell split in several ways. One may add hidden variables and accept Bell's no-go consequences as constraints on which such theories survive. One may add collapse or other dynamics. One may add branch structure or observer-relative bookkeeping. One may preserve operational quantum theory and treat the correlations as primitive. The present theorem does not choose among those constructive or interpretive programs. It supplies the constraint that any same-domain Bell-witness account must meet. If a proposal treats Bell data as a reusable witness object, then setting/outcome interfaces, channel roles, correlator content, no-signaling marginals, and admissible reuse must remain fixed. If a proposed mechanism, interpretation, or reconstruction violates those requirements, it does not preserve the same Bell target.

\paragraph{Why this target makes the kernel roles visible.}
Bell-scope practice supplies an ideal target for the kernel roles because it displays the precise gap between physical correlation occurrence and non-degenerate witness objecthood. Quantum theory and experiment supply physical Bell correlations. The Bell witness role adds sameness across settings, stable witness-channel identity, standing-bearing reuse, and lawful reparameterization. That gap is where the kernel roles become load-bearing. Reference fixes the same witness object; standing licenses reuse as Bell evidence; admissibility separates witness-preserving transformations from hidden load and scope change; irreversibility blocks delayed calibration or post-selection from repairing the same witness act.

The target is not merely taxonomic. It is a determinacy theorem: Bell-witness objecthood is kernel-governed. The surviving boundary-level compatibility normal form is not an optional interpretive gloss. It is the terminal same-domain determinacy normal form once locally sufficient standing, hidden variables, signaling, mechanism-role reclassification, repair, delayed calibration, non-trace-mediated selectors, and scope transport are closed.

\paragraph{Handoff from the measurement-record theorem.}
The companion measurement-record paper establishes the record side of the paired arc: physical record occurrence is not enough for non-degenerate evidential record objecthood; admissible record-role fixation is required. The present paper establishes the witness side: physical Bell correlation occurrence is not enough for non-degenerate Bell-witness objecthood; boundary-level compatibility carried by a joint standing class is required. The handoff is direct. Records are the retained evidential outputs through which experiments are used; Bell witnesses are the setting/outcome probability structures those records support. The same fixed-domain discipline governs both.

\paragraph{Positive Bell-side explanation: AMetric co-standing.}
An entangled preparation is admitted as one joint standing class, not as two independently complete local standing states. In physical-facing terms, the entangled preparation is the standing-bearing object, not two independent local objects plus a later connector. The two wings co-stand at the AMetric boundary in the precise admissibility sense: their tensor compatibility is fixed before metric distance, temporal order, signal transport, or propagation speed become admissibility-relevant. Here ``before'' denotes admissibility-priority, not temporal precedence. Local measurements later realize compatible local faces of that one class through declared setting/outcome interfaces. The family of realized outcome pairs carries Bell correlation structure by shared boundary-cofixed standing, not by communication.

\section{Bell scope, physical occurrence, and witness determinacy}\label{sec:scope}

\subsection{The fixed Bell scope}
We work with a fixed Bell scope $\scope_{\Bell}$ containing:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2em]
    \item a bipartite preparation represented in the standard quantum formalism by a density operator $\rho_{AB}$ on $\HS_A\otimes\HS_B$;
    \item local measurement wings with settings $x\in X$ on side $A$ and $y\in Y$ on side $B$;
    \item outcome variables $a\in A_x$ and $b\in B_y$;
    \item operational joint probabilities $P(a,b\mid x,y)$ and correlators $E_{xy}$;
    \item the no-signaling constraints
    \[
    \sum_b P(a,b\mid x,y)=\sum_b P(a,b\mid x,y')\quad \forall a,x,y,y',
    \]
    \[
    \sum_a P(a,b\mid x,y)=\sum_a P(a,b\mid x',y)\quad \forall b,y,x,x';
    \]
    \item when appropriate, the CHSH expression
    \[
    \CHSH=E_{00}+E_{01}+E_{10}-E_{11}.
    \]
\end{enumerate}
Bell-local theories satisfy $|\CHSH|\le 2$, while quantum theory permits violations for suitable states and settings \cite{Bell1964,CHSH1969,Brunner2014}.

\begin{definition}[Physical Bell correlation occurrence]
A physical Bell correlation occurrence is the physical and operational occurrence of a Bell-type correlation table, including preparations, settings, outcomes, detector events, records, estimated probabilities, and no-signaling marginals. It may be supplied by ordinary quantum theory and experiment. By itself it is not yet a non-degenerate Bell witness object.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Ontic non-degenerate Bell-witness determinacy]\label{def:ndbell}
A Bell operational table $P(a,b\mid x,y)$ has ontic non-degenerate Bell-witness determinacy, written $\NDBell(P)$, iff it functions as the same witness-bearing object under:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2em]
    \item stable target identity across lawful relabeling and reparameterization;
    \item fixed setting/outcome, detector, calibration, and coincidence interfaces;
    \item standing-bearing reuse in Bell comparison, record retention, and later classification;
    \item admissible distinction between witness-preserving and witness-altering transformations;
    \item act-time finality against delayed repair or post-selected channel privilege;
    \item preservation of tensor-level compatibility content under the same Bell scope.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Physical occurrence is not yet Bell-witness determinacy]
For a Bell table $P$, physical Bell correlation occurrence does not by itself imply ontic non-degenerate Bell-witness determinacy:
\[
\PhysBell(P)\not\Rightarrow \NDBell(P).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Physical occurrence supplies the operational material: preparation events, detector events, setting choices, outcome records, and estimated probabilities. Bell-witness determinacy adds a standing role: the table must remain the same witness object across setting comparison, lawful re-expression, channel fixation, retained records, and later classification. A raw table may occur without fixing which channels, calibration links, readout identifications, or coincidence rules constitute the witness-bearing object. It may also occur without a standing rule distinguishing support, bookkeeping, target shift, or selector import. Hence occurrence alone does not supply non-degenerate witness determinacy.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Boundary traces for Bell witness channels}

\begin{definition}[Bell boundary trace]\label{def:belltrace}
A Bell boundary trace is a declared fixed-domain predicate
\[
\Trace_{Ch}:S^{ch}_{\Bell}\to\{0,1\}
\]
on the candidate setting/outcome interfaces, detector channels, calibration links, readout identifications, or coincidence-window identifications relevant to the Bell witness set. Its valuation is declared before Bell-scope classification, stable under lawful identity-preserving transformations of the Bell domain, bivalent, outcome-independent, and non-generative. It fixes which interfaces count as witness-bearing channels on the evaluated domain. It does not create Bell correlations, alter the Hilbert-space formalism, add detector ontology, or decide the Bell value after the fact.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[No arbitrary Bell singleton trace]\label{thm:noarbitrarytrace}
A singleton-valued predicate on candidate Bell channels fixes a Bell witness-channel role only if its trace source is independently declared as part of the Bell-domain architecture before Bell-scope classification. If the predicate is chosen after observing the data, conditioned on the Bell result, scalarized by a discretionary threshold, or introduced to privilege one channel among otherwise admissible presentations, then it is delayed repair, post-selection, hidden selector import, or scope change rather than same-domain witness fixation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A witness-channel trace performs admissibility-relevant anchoring work. If the source of that trace is not part of the declared Bell domain before classification, then the trace does not preserve the witness object; it selects or repairs it after the relevant act. A singleton predicate chosen after the data can always be manufactured and therefore cannot by itself carry standing. Outcome-conditioning makes the trace a desired-result selector. A scalar threshold makes boundary status metric or ranked rather than bivalent. A post-classification privilege changes the channel package on which witness identity depends. Each route violates the fixed-domain requirement that witness-preserving interfaces be declared before standing-bearing use. Hence singleton-valuedness is necessary for singular channel fixation when a trace is used, but not sufficient. The trace source must be predeclared, stable, bivalent, outcome-independent, and non-generative.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bell boundary-trace admissibility]\label{thm:belltraceadmiss}
Detector, setting, channel, calibration, coincidence, or apparatus facts are admissible Bell-scope anchors only when they enter as declared stable Bell boundary traces in the sense of \cref{def:belltrace}. Otherwise they are support, bookkeeping, delayed repair, hidden selector import, or scope change.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A Bell-scope anchor must preserve the witness set rather than choose among candidate witness sets after classification has begun. Declaration blocks after-the-fact channel selection. Bivalence blocks graded channel legitimacy from functioning as a hidden metric at the boundary. Stability blocks relabeling or calibration drift from changing the target object under identity-preserving transformation. Outcome independence blocks selection of the channel because it supports a desired Bell value. Non-generativity blocks the trace from creating witness standing for itself. Failure of any condition either changes the evaluated domain, imports extra selector authority, or repairs a failed witness role as the same act. Hence only declared stable boundary traces can carry Bell-scope anchoring work.
\end{proof}

\section{Joint standing classes and tensor-level compatibility}\label{sec:jointstanding}

\begin{definition}[Bell-scope standing equivalence]
Fix $\scope_{\Bell}$. Two presentations $p,p'$ of the preparation are Bell-scope standing-equivalent, written $p\sim_{\Bell}p'$, iff they agree on all Bell-scope observables, no-signaling marginals, correlators, and admitted setting/outcome compatibility content. The quotient class
\[
[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}
\]
is the Bell-scope standing class of the preparation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Entangled joint standing class]
An entangled joint standing class is a Bell-scope standing class whose Bell witness set cannot be factored into a pair of independently sufficient local standing classes while preserving all admitted joint observables and no-signaling marginals.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Tensor-level compatibility]
Tensor-level compatibility is the standing-bearing quotient-level relation among admitted joint observables that is preserved by the Bell witness set but not reducible to independently sufficient local standing packages on the same Bell scope.
\end{definition}


\begin{definition}[Bell-scope tensor-compatibility content]\label{def:tb}
For an admitted Bell-scope standing class $[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}$, write $T_B=T_B([p]_{\sim_{\Bell}})$ for its Bell-scope tensor-compatibility content. This is the quotient-level standing-bearing structure that fixes, for each admitted setting pair $(x,y)$, the admissible joint realization profile and associated operational probabilities $P(a,b\mid x,y)$ while preserving the no-signaling marginals. $T_B$ is not a list of pre-existing local values, not a hidden variable, and not a deterministic assignment of an outcome pair for every unperformed setting.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Joint standing class as Bell determinacy normal form]\label{prop:jscnormal}
Within the fixed Bell scope, the admissible determinacy normal form of an entangled preparation is a joint standing class carrying tensor-level compatibility. It is not a pair of independently complete local standing states plus later influence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The standing-bearing Bell data are not exhausted by the separate local marginals. Bell-violating correlations preserve joint constraints that survive lawful re-expression while failing Bell-local factorization. By anchor/tensor/skin discipline, the load-bearing content that survives quotient-level reuse is tensor content rather than skin. Because that content links admitted joint observables across settings, it is compatibility content. If one attempts to distribute that content into independently sufficient local standing classes, the resulting structure either fails Bell violation, imports hidden load, or changes scope. Thus the same-domain normal form is a joint standing class carrying tensor-level compatibility.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Independence is not a neutral default]
Within the Bell scope, decomposition of an entangled preparation into two independently sufficient local standing classes is not a neutral starting point. It is an additional same-scope claim that must itself be licensed.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
A decomposition into independently sufficient local standing classes asserts that the Bell witness set is generated without irreducible joint tensor compatibility. That assertion changes the standing-bearing typing of the witness. If the decomposition preserves the full Bell witness set without extra load, then it merely restates the same joint tensor in skin-level form. If it introduces new content to recover Bell violation, then it adds hidden load or changes scope. No presumption of local independence survives the fixed Bell witness once Bell violation and no-signaling are held fixed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local standing completeness contradiction]\label{thm:localstandingcontradiction}
Assume Bell-violating, no-signaling data on the fixed Bell scope admit a same-domain classification in which each wing carries independently sufficient local standing for the admitted settings. Then identity-preserving reuse across settings requires joint compatibility content not recoverable from those independently sufficient local standings alone. Hence independently sufficient local standing contradicts the fixed Bell witness set as a same-domain reusable object.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If each wing carries independently sufficient local standing, then the standing-bearing contribution to each outcome pair is exhausted by wingwise authorizer content. No further joint compatibility resource may be invoked without abandoning the assumption of independent sufficiency. Reuse across the CHSH family, however, requires one Bell witness object linking the four setting pairs. Bell-violating data contain cross-setting compatibility load not recoverable from the separate local marginals and not capturable by wingwise authorizer content alone; otherwise the data would admit Bell-local standing and satisfy the Bell-local bound. Therefore either the local standing remains genuinely local and fails to recover the Bell witness, or it recovers the witness only by silently importing joint compatibility. In the latter case it is no longer independently sufficient local standing. The assumption fails on the original Bell scope.
\end{proof}

\section{AMetric co-standing and boundary co-fixation}\label{sec:costanding}
The preceding section identified an entangled preparation with a joint standing class rather than with two independently complete local standing states. The present section spells out the determinacy structure of that claim. The two Bell wings do not first stand as independent local objects and then acquire correlation by communication. They co-stand as local roles of one admitted joint standing class. Their compatibility is fixed at the AMetric boundary before metric distance, temporal order, signal transport, or causal propagation can function as admissibility-relevant discriminators. Here and below, \emph{before} denotes admissibility-priority rather than temporal precedence: metric distance and temporal order are licensed interior descriptors downstream of boundary fixation. This use of the AMetric boundary follows the boundary theorem that no metric, order, ranking, probability, selector, canonical representative, or boundary-transmissive operation can carry admissibility-bearing authority at admission \cite{MaleyAMetricBoundary2026}.

\begin{definition}[Local Bell face]\label{def:localbellface}
Let $[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}$ be a Bell-scope joint standing class. For a local setting $x$ on wing $A$, the $A$-local face of $[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}$ at $x$ is the admissible role through which a local outcome $a\in A_x$ may be realized from the joint standing class through the declared setting/outcome interface on wing $A$. The $B$-local face at setting $y$ is defined analogously. A local face is not an independently sufficient local standing state. It is a local realization role of the joint class.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[AMetric co-standing]\label{def:ametriccostanding}
The two wings of a Bell preparation are in AMetric co-standing iff they are admitted not as two independently complete local standing states, but as local roles of one joint standing class $[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}$ whose tensor-level compatibility is fixed at the AMetric boundary. AMetric co-standing means that the standing-bearing compatibility of the two wings is fixed before metric distance, temporal order, signal transport, or propagation speed can function as admissibility-relevant discriminators, where before is an admissibility-priority relation rather than a temporal relation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Boundary co-fixation]\label{def:boundarycofixation}
Boundary co-fixation is the admission-level fixation of the joint standing class and its tensor-level compatibility relation. It fixes the compatibility structure sampled by local measurements. It does not transmit information between wings, assign pre-existing local values, generate a distant state, or repair a distant state after a local act. On the Bell scope, boundary co-fixation is mediated by declared setting/outcome, detector, calibration, readout, or coincidence traces. Such traces are admissible only as declared, stable, bivalent, channel-preserving predicates inside the target domain, not as environmental oracles or post-hoc selectors \cite{MaleyBoundaryTrace2026}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Admissible realization pair]\label{def:realizationpair}
For settings $(x,y)$ and a joint standing class $[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}$ carrying tensor compatibility $T_B$, write
\[
\Real_{x,y}([p]_{\sim_{\Bell}},T_B) \subseteq A_x\times B_y
\]
for the class of local outcome pairs admissible as joint realizations of the boundary-cofixed compatibility structure at those settings. Membership $(a,b)\in\Real_{x,y}([p]_{\sim_{\Bell}},T_B)$ says that $a$ and $b$ are compatible local faces of the same joint standing class. The family of such realization pairs carries the Bell correlation structure. The notation does not assign pre-existing values to unperformed measurements and does not make $T_B$ a deterministic local-value selector.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[AMetric co-standing yields correlation without communication]\label{thm:costandingcorr}
Let $[p]_{\sim_{\Bell}}$ be an admitted Bell-scope joint standing class carrying tensor-level compatibility $T_B$. Suppose local measurements at settings $x$ and $y$ are admissible local realizations through declared Bell boundary traces. Then each realized pair $(a,b)$ is an admissible local realization pair for $T_B$, equivalently $(a,b)\in\Real_{x,y}([p]_{\sim_{\Bell}},T_B)$, and the family of such realization pairs carries the Bell correlation structure. $T_B$ constrains the joint realization profile in the standing-bearing compatibility sense; it does not determine pre-assigned local values for unperformed settings. The correlation arises from shared realization of the same boundary-cofixed joint standing class. It is not produced by communication, metric transport, distant-state repair, or same-scope standing transfer between independently complete local states.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By the definition of an entangled joint standing class, the Bell-scope preparation is admitted as one standing-bearing object whose irreducible content is tensor-level compatibility. By AMetric co-standing, the local wings are not admitted as independently complete local standing states. They are local roles of the same joint standing class.

The declared setting/outcome interfaces on the two wings determine how local measurements may realize representatives of that joint class. A local measurement at setting $x$ realizes an $A$-local face; a local measurement at setting $y$ realizes a $B$-local face. Because both local faces belong to the same admitted joint standing class, each admissible realization pair belongs to the joint realization profile fixed by the tensor compatibility $T_B$ carried by that class. Across the family of such realization pairs, this profile is what carries the Bell correlation structure.

No communication is required for this constraint to hold. Communication would require a same-domain act on one wing to transmit standing-bearing content, modulate the distant marginal, alter the distant boundary trace, change the joint tensor after admission, or repair the standing of the distant wing. These routes are excluded by the AMetric boundary, standing conservation, boundary-trace stability, irreversibility, and the anchor/tensor/skin role discipline. Therefore the correlation structure is compatibility-realization within one boundary-cofixed standing class, not communication between two independently standing local systems.
\end{proof}

The determinacy chain is:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{entangled preparation} &\Rightarrow [p]_{\sim_{\Bell}} \\
&\Rightarrow \text{AMetric co-standing} \Rightarrow T_B,\\
T_B &\Rightarrow \text{local realization profile }\Real_{x,y},\\
\Real_{x,y} &\Rightarrow \text{Bell correlation without communication}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Individual outcome pairs are local realizations of one boundary-cofixed compatibility structure; the family of realization pairs carries the Bell correlation structure. That structure is not produced by a signal passing from one wing to the other.

\section{Measurement as admissible local realization}\label{sec:measurement}

\begin{definition}[Local measurement act]
A local measurement act in the Bell scope is an admissible same-scope act that realizes an outcome representative relative to a chosen local measurement context on one wing of the already admitted joint standing class.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Local measurement realizes, not produces, Bell compatibility]\label{thm:localmeasurement}
A local Bell measurement samples the AMetrically co-standing joint standing class through a fixed local context. It realizes a local face of the boundary-cofixed compatibility structure. It does not create, transmit, repair, or change tensor-level compatibility on the same scope.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A same-scope tensor-changing operation would have to create standing, transfer standing, amplify standing, or repair incomplete standing. Standing conservation and irreversibility exclude those routes on the fixed domain. The role-discipline results also block treating tensor content as an operator or mechanism. A local measurement may generate a local outcome record, but the compatibility structure sampled by that record is not generated by the local act. The act realizes a local face inside the admitted joint standing lineage. If paired with a local act on the other wing, the realized pair belongs to the joint realization profile fixed by $T_B$ as in \cref{thm:costandingcorr}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Local realization requires fixed witness-channel anchoring]\label{prop:localchannel}
A local Bell measurement can realize the joint tensor only through a setting/outcome channel already fixed as part of the Bell witness domain. The local act does not determine the witness role after the fact; it samples admitted compatibility through a boundary-trace-anchored local interface.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{thm:belltraceadmiss}, setting/outcome and detector interfaces can serve as same-domain anchors only through declared stable boundary traces. A local measurement act therefore has an admitted channel through which a representative is realized, but it has no authority to decide after the fact which channel counted as witness-bearing. If channel identity is chosen after observing the result, the move is repair or selector import. Hence local realization requires prior witness-channel anchoring.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[No delayed Bell calibration repair]\label{prop:nodelayedrepair}
Delayed calibration, post-selected detector identification, later coincidence-window choice, posterior channel privilege, or retrospective setting/outcome relabeling cannot repair Bell-scope classification on the same domain. If such material is added after witness fixation fails or after the witness role is already used, it defines a fresh Bell domain or imports an inadmissible same-scope selector.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
A calibration or coincidence rule may be legitimate when declared in advance as a stable boundary trace. If introduced later to decide which channel or data slice counts as the Bell witness, it changes the channel package on which witness identity depends. Treating the later package as if it fixed the earlier same act is same-act repair. Treating it explicitly as new declared structure is a scope reset. Neither is same-domain repair of the original Bell classification.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No same-act repair typing of collapse]
A collapse typing that requires the local act on one wing to repair, complete, or finish an otherwise standing-incomplete distant state is inadmissible on the same Bell scope.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Such a typing makes the distant state standing-complete only after the local act. That is same-scope repair or completion. Irreversibility and no-repair closure exclude same-act repair on the fixed domain. Therefore the typing is inadmissible as same-domain Bell classification.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Handoff to record determinacy.}
The local act may produce a local record occurrence. Whether that record occurrence stands as the same evidential object for retained reuse is the target of the companion measurement-record paper. The present theorem governs the witness compatibility sampled by such records; the record theorem governs the evidential standing of the records themselves.

\section{Why this is not hidden variables}\label{sec:hiddenvariables}

\begin{definition}[Bell-local value determinacy]
A Bell-local value-determinacy presentation is a same-scope model assigning pre-existing local values or local response functions to the admitted measurement settings through a factorization of the form
\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)=\int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)P(a\mid x,\lambda)P(b\mid y,\lambda).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Compatibility determinacy is not local value determinacy]\label{prop:notHV}
The Bell-scope joint standing class fixes compatibility constraints, not pre-assigned local values for all possible settings. It is therefore not a hidden-variable completion of the Bell-local type.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
A Bell-local hidden-variable model supplies local response structure indexed by $\lambda$. The joint standing class supplies equality of admitted joint observables and compatibility relations among them. It does not assign standing values to unperformed measurements. Once a context is chosen, the resulting outcome pair belongs to the compatibility structure of the admitted joint class. A value assignment is stronger than a compatibility relation. Bell's theorem excludes Bell-local value assignment for Bell-violating quantum statistics; the present classification does not reintroduce it.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bell-local factorization is not an admissible same-scope realization]\label{thm:belllocalinadmissible}
Suppose Bell-scope operational data are Bell-violating and no-signaling. Any same-domain realization that attempts to classify those data by Bell-local factorization must do at least one of the following:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2em]
    \item fail extensionally by Bell's theorem;
    \item add standing-bearing auxiliary load not licensed by the Bell witness set;
    \item reclassify quotient-level tensor compatibility as locally distributed authorizer structure;
    \item change scope.
\end{enumerate}
Hence Bell-local factorization is not an admissible same-scope determinacy normal form for Bell-violating data.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If a standard Bell-local factorization reproduces Bell-violating data, Bell's theorem is contradicted. If the factorization is rescued by adding auxiliary structure not present in the admitted witness, that structure performs hidden standing work and is hidden load. If no auxiliary load is claimed, then the factorization can only recover the data by treating joint compatibility as if it were locally distributed authorizer content; that is a inadmissible role reclassification under anchor/tensor/skin discipline. If the proposal changes the witness object, admissibility conditions, or evaluated domain, it is scope change. These cases exhaust the same-domain factorization route.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Hidden variables as extra-domain asymmetry unless inert]
A Bell-local hidden variable is not a new admissible same-domain asymmetry source. If it is extensionally inert, it is skin-level bookkeeping over the joint standing class. If it performs standing-bearing work not fixed by the Bell witness set, it is hidden load or scope change.
\end{corollary}

\section{No-signaling endpoint: compatibility is not transport}\label{sec:nosignaling}

The no-signaling endpoint depends on AMetric co-standing. The local outcomes form Bell correlations because the two local measurements realize compatible local faces of one boundary-cofixed joint class. They are not correlated by communication between independently complete local states. The numerical no-signaling equalities are part of the fixed witness input; the theorem below concerns the exclusion of same-domain signaling, transport, marginal-modulation, and distant-repair typings.

\begin{definition}[Signaling locality]
A Bell-scope presentation satisfies signaling locality iff the local marginals on one wing are invariant under admissible changes of measurement setting on the other wing.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Bell-scope signaling typing]
A Bell-scope signaling typing is a same-domain classification under which a local setting choice, local measurement act, local outcome, or local record on one wing changes standing-bearing content on the other wing in a way that could modulate the distant marginal or carry endpoint-relevant information across the metric separation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Metric transport and distant-repair typings]
A metric transport typing classifies Bell correlation as a carrier, signal, influence, causal process, or standing-bearing transfer between spacelike separated wings. A distant-standing repair typing classifies a local act on one wing as completing, repairing, or conferring standing on the distant wing as the same Bell-scope act.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[No same-domain marginal modulation]\label{lem:channelinvariance}
On a fixed Bell domain, changing the remote setting cannot alter the local boundary trace that fixes the local setting/outcome channel. Therefore no admissible same-scope act can modulate the distant marginal by changing the distant wing's witness-channel identity, local admissibility status, or local marginal role.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A Bell boundary trace is declared before classification and stable under lawful identity-preserving transformation. If a remote setting change altered the local trace value, local witness-channel identity would not be preserved on the same Bell domain. The remote setting would function as a channel selector. If a remote act altered the local admissibility status or marginal role, it would introduce a second same-domain discriminator or repair the witness role after the fact. Those routes violate boundary-trace admissibility, same-scope closure, and irreversibility. Hence local trace status, local admissibility status, and local marginal role remain invariant under remote setting changes on the fixed scope.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[No-signaling endpoint theorem: no same-domain signaling typing]\label{thm:compatnosig}
Let $P$ be Bell-violating witness content used as a non-degenerate Bell object on the fixed Bell scope. Under the active kernel roles and boundary-trace channel fixation, no same-domain signaling typing of $P$ is admissible. The correlations are typed as boundary-level tensor compatibility of a joint standing class, not as signal, metric transport, distant-standing repair, or marginal-modulating influence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A same-domain signaling typing would have to supply at least one of four forms of standing-bearing change: marginal modulation, tensor-content change after admission, distant standing repair, or a carrier transporting standing-bearing information. \Cref{lem:channelinvariance} blocks marginal modulation through witness-channel identity, local admissibility status, or local marginal role. Standing conservation blocks creation, transfer, or amplification of tensor content after admission. Irreversibility blocks distant-state repair as the same Bell-scope act. Anchor/tensor/skin role discipline blocks conversion of tensor compatibility into an undeclared carrier or mechanism. By \cref{thm:costandingcorr}, the Bell correlation structure is already accounted for as compatibility-realization within one boundary-cofixed joint standing class. A signaling or transport typing is therefore not needed to explain correlation; it would add a second same-domain standing-bearing route. The remaining same-domain description is that both local acts sample already fixed compatibility through their admitted contexts. Correlation without controllable marginal modulation, transport, or distant repair is not signaling. Therefore signaling typing is unavailable on the fixed Bell scope.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No superluminal communication as a same-domain corollary]
Within the fixed Bell scope, an entangled correlation cannot be used as a superluminal communication channel.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{thm:compatnosig}, no admissible local act modulates the distant marginal, transports standing-bearing content across the metric separation, or repairs distant standing as the same act. Without controllable distant modulation or transport, there is no communication channel. This is the communication-level expression of boundary-level compatibility, not a separate locality postulate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bell violation and no-signaling force the boundary-compatibility normal form]\label{thm:bellnosigcompat}
On the fixed Bell scope, Bell violation together with the no-signaling endpoint forces boundary-level tensor compatibility rather than transport. Bell violation excludes independently sufficient local standing and local value-completion; no-signaling endpoint closure excludes metric transport, signaling, and distant repair; same-scope closure excludes extra discriminators. The surviving determinacy normal form is a joint standing class carrying tensor-level compatibility.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Bell violation closes the route on which the witness is carried by independently sufficient local standing. The no-signaling endpoint theorem closes the route on which the same witness is carried by signal, transport, marginal modulation, or distant repair. The closure spine closes hidden same-scope discriminators and same-act repairs. Boundary-trace discipline fixes witness channels without allowing detector or apparatus facts to become post-hoc selectors. Once these alternatives are closed, the only remaining same-domain determinacy form is the joint-standing-class tensor-compatibility normal form.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{No-signaling as a determinacy endpoint.}
The theorem does not derive the numerical no-signaling equations from AASC alone; those equations are part of the fixed Bell witness set. It proves the no-signaling endpoint relevant to Bell-witness determinacy: once that no-signaling witness is fixed as the evaluated object, no same-domain construction can reinterpret the correlations as signal, transport, marginal-modulating influence, or distant repair without changing scope or importing hidden load. Nonlocal correlation, formal state update, or relational bookkeeping may remain available at their proper scope; what is excluded is their same-domain typing as metric transport of standing-bearing Bell-witness content. Bell violation marks the failure of independently sufficient local standing; AMetric co-standing explains how local outcomes can nevertheless be correlated as faces of one boundary-cofixed class; no-signaling endpoint closure marks the absence of admissible standing transfer.

\section{AMetric boundary and apparent instantaneousness}\label{sec:ametric}
The standard puzzle of entanglement is not merely that correlations exist, but that they look instantaneous in metric bookkeeping. The present theorem does not posit an additional transport mechanism. It identifies where metric description enters and where it cannot do admissibility-relevant work.
AMetric co-standing supplies the missing determinacy picture. The wings co-stand, rather than spatially coexist, at the AMetric boundary: the joint class is admitted before metric distance and temporal order become admissibility-relevant descriptors, where before marks admissibility-priority rather than temporal priority. Apparent instantaneousness is therefore the interior metric trace of boundary-cofixed compatibility, not evidence of a hidden propagating influence.

\begin{theorem}[Boundary-level fixation]\label{thm:boundarylevel}
The tensor compatibility relation carried by an entangled joint standing class is fixed at admission, and admission is governed by the AMetric boundary. Therefore the standing-bearing source of Bell correlation is fixed at a level at which metric distance and time are not admissibility-relevant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Standing is fixed at admission. Tensor-level compatibility, formalized here as $T_B$, is standing-bearing content of the joint class. The AMetric boundary is the closure-spine result that pre-admissible boundary status is not governed by metric distance, time, ranking, probability, selector, canonical representative, or boundary-transmissive operation \cite{MaleyAMetricBoundary2026}. Hence compatibility is fixed before interior metric descriptors can carry standing-bearing authority. Metric distance and temporal order may later describe measurement events inside the admitted domain; they do not generate the compatibility. The word ``before'' is used here in the same admissibility-priority sense as in \cref{def:ametriccostanding}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Apparent instantaneousness is interior bookkeeping]\label{thm:instant}
If compatibility is fixed at the AMetric boundary and later represented in a metric interior with spacelike-separated detections, then the correlation appears instantaneous from the interior point of view without any transmitted influence existing at the boundary level.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The interior description introduces coordinates, spacetime separation, and temporal order as licensed descriptors inside an admitted regime. Those descriptors organize standing-bearing content; they do not create it. When joint compatibility is sampled at separated locations, the interior representation displays the compatibility as holding across separation. Because compatibility was not generated by interior transport, no superluminal influence is needed. Apparent instantaneousness is the metric trace of boundary-cofixed compatibility. It is the spacetime presentation of AMetric co-standing, not the trace of a signal.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The AMetric boundary is not a physical distance scale]
Boundary-level compatibility does not identify the AMetric boundary with the Planck scale or any other interior metric cutoff.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The AMetric boundary is defined by absence of admissibility-relevant metric structure, not by an extreme value of metric structure. A Planck-scale description is still metric and therefore interior.
\end{proof}

\section{CHSH audit and terminal Bell classification}\label{sec:chsh}
The CHSH audit is where Bell-witness determinacy becomes visible in compact form: the witness table cannot be carried by independently sufficient local standing while preserving the same Bell object.

\subsection{Input and output skins}
The Hilbert-space presentation represents the preparation by $\rho_{AB}$ and local measurement operators. The operational presentation represents the same Bell-scope content by $P(a,b\mid x,y)$, local marginals, and correlators $E_{xy}$. The Born rule transports Hilbert presentation to operational witness table:
\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)=\mathrm{Tr}\bigl[\rho_{AB}(M^{A}_{a\mid x}\otimes M^{B}_{b\mid y})\bigr].
\]
This transport is complete for the Bell scope because the operational table is the witness object whose determinacy is under audit.

\begin{proposition}[CHSH incompatibility of local standing sufficiency]\label{prop:chshstanding}
Fix Bell-scope data with $|\CHSH|>2$ and no-signaling marginals. If outcomes on the fixed Bell scope were generated from independently sufficient local standing under identity-preserving reuse across settings, then the induced correlators would satisfy $|\CHSH|\le 2$. Therefore Bell-violating data are incompatible with locally sufficient standing on the original Bell scope.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Independently sufficient local standing leaves no standing-bearing place for irreducible cross-setting compatibility. The correlators must be determined by wingwise standing data alone. That is the structural content of the Bell-local bound in the CHSH setting. Therefore $|\CHSH|\le 2$. Bell-violating data contradict this. To preserve the data one must import joint compatibility or hidden load. If joint compatibility is imported, local standing was not independently sufficient; if hidden load is imported, the proposal is not a same-domain local-standing classification.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Terminal Bell classification classes]\label{def:terminalclasses}
Under the fixed Bell scope, each proposal receives a terminal standing-role classification by the following priority order:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(F\arabic*),leftmargin=2.8em]
    \item scope change: the witness set, admissibility envelope, or standing authority is altered;
    \item delayed repair: later calibration, coincidence choice, channel privilege, or retrospective relabeling repairs an unfixed witness;
    \item non-trace selector: detector, apparatus, or environment facts choose the witness without a declared stable boundary trace;
    \item hidden load: an auxiliary variable, carrier, or discriminator performs standing-bearing work not fixed by the witness;

\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Terminal classification exhaustion]\label{lem:terminalexhaust}
Every same-domain Bell-scope proposal receives a terminal standing-role classification under \cref{def:terminalclasses}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A proposal either preserves the witness domain or alters it. If it alters it, it is scope change. If it preserves it but relies on later channel choice or calibration, it is delayed repair. If it uses detector or apparatus facts without a declared trace, it is a non-trace selector. If it adds auxiliary standing-bearing content, it is hidden load. If it treats compatibility as mechanism, it is mechanism-role reclassification. If it appeals to the apparatus globally without specifying fixed channels, it is global apparatus authority. If none of these applies, the proposal preserves the witness through fixed interfaces without additional standing-bearing load; it is boundary-compatible determinacy. The priority order handles overlap and gives one terminal classification.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Collapse to the Bell-witness determinacy normal form]\label{thm:classcollapse}

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
