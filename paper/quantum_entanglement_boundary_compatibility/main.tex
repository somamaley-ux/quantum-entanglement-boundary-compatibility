\section{Introduction}
Quantum entanglement presents a familiar structural split. Bell and CHSH show that suitable operational correlations cannot be reproduced by Bell-local hidden-variable factorization, while the same operational tables satisfy no-signaling constraints and cannot be used for controllable superluminal communication \cite{Bell1964,CHSH1969,Brunner2014}. The present manuscript asks a different but prior question: what must be fixed when that table is used as the same non-degenerate Bell witness across settings, lawful relabelings, retained records, and later classification?

The theorem does not choose a mechanism generating the correlations. It identifies the standing-bearing form of the reusable witness. Physical occurrence supplies preparations, settings, detector events, outcomes, records, and estimated probabilities. Bell-witness determinacy adds governed sameness: one fixed operational family, one declared interface package, one lawful transformation class, one act-time status, and one licensed reuse profile. Once that target is instantiated, the kernel roles are identity conditions rather than optional terminology, and the target already occupies an admissible governance form. The proof then concerns rigidity: no second governance-non-equivalent form may faithfully realize the same determinate object.

The mathematical carrier is a finite Bell empirical model: a family of context distributions on the measurement cover whose restrictions agree on overlaps. Bell-local completion is the existence of a global joint distribution or, equivalently in the finite setting, a convex mixture of deterministic global assignments. Fine established the CHSH/global-joint-distribution equivalence in the binary scenario, while the sheaf-theoretic framework identifies Bell nonlocality as obstruction to a global section on the measurement cover \cite{Fine1982,AbramskyBrandenburger2011}. This manuscript uses that established mathematics to construct a Bell joint obstruction packet and an exact realization family before defining the compatibility object.

The locality vocabulary is kept disciplined. Jarrett and Shimony distinguish locality conditions often discussed as parameter independence and outcome independence \cite{Jarrett1984,Shimony1986}. The present no-signaling hierarchy does not replace that analysis: it separates operational marginal invariance, absence of a controllable communication channel, and exclusion of same-domain standing-transport typing. Operational-equivalence and ontological-model analyses likewise show why identity of procedures and representations matters when moving from observed statistics to underlying models \cite{Spekkens2005}. Causal-model work demonstrates that no-signaling Bell violations impose nontrivial constraints on causal explanations and may require fine-tuning under standard causal assumptions \cite{WoodSpekkens2015,Cavalcanti2018}. Those richer-scope formalisms are not excluded. The semantic representation theorem classifies only the governance effect they claim on the fixed witness: anchor admission, standing content, admissible continuation, act-time status, target identity, or no independent effect.

No-signaling correlations extend beyond the quantum set. Popescu--Rohrlich and device-independent analyses exhibit Bell-nonlocal no-signaling models that are not quantum-realizable \cite{PopescuRohrlich1994,BarrettEtAl2005}. Conversely, entanglement alone does not guarantee Bell nonlocality; Werner states supply the standard separation \cite{Werner1989}. Accordingly, the strongest theorem below is first stated for finite Bell-nonlocal no-signaling empirical models. The quantum specialization adds a Hilbert-space realization; separable realizations are Bell-local, so Bell violation forces the realizing state to be entangled within the specified carrier.

The released Lean companion mechanizes a concrete finite carrier. It derives an exact denominator-$32$ vector/Born table from integer geometry, proves normalization and structural no-signaling, obtains CHSH numerator $80$, classifies all sixteen deterministic local assignments, and excludes the witness from the denominator-$32$ Bell-local envelope. The manuscript supplies the distinct structural layer: target occupancy, semantic governance representation, extensional governance equivalence, the Bell joint obstruction packet, exact compatibility certification, positive target-active componentwise uniqueness, and the separation between the general Bell-nonlocal result and the quantum-entanglement corollary.

\paragraph{Positive Bell-side interpretation.}
The numerical values come from the operational or quantum carrier. AASC supplies the admissible same-domain type of the object carrying them. The local wings are not treated as independently complete standing states later joined by a message. They are local roles of one compatible empirical model, fixed through declared interfaces and sampled context by context. ``AMetric co-standing'' names this admission-level relation; it is not a hidden surface, an influence, or a derivation of the probability values.

The present paper governs the standing structure of the Bell witness sampled by local records. The companion paper governs whether those records can be reused as the same evidential objects. The two results are coordinated but logically distinct.

\begin{definition}[Finite operational Bell object]\label{def:operationalbellobject}
Fix ambient sets of target identifiers, act identifiers, status values, comparison tests, report tokens, and finite presentation labels, together with an act-support map
\[
\ell^{\rm act}:\mathsf{Act}\to\{A,B,AB\}.
\]
A finite operational Bell object is a tuple
\[
\begin{aligned}
O=(&X_O,Y_O,\{A^O_x\},\{B^O_y\},P_O,\mathcal C_O,\ell_O^{C},\alpha_O,\theta_O,
\mathsf Z_O,\sigma_O,\ell_O^{Z},\eta_O,\mathsf S_O,\ell_O^{S},\mathcal K_O,\\
&\mathsf{Rpt}_O,\ell_O^{R},\rho_O,\zeta_O,\mathsf W_O,\omega_O,\tau_O),
\end{aligned}
\]
where $X_O,Y_O$ and all outcome sets are finite; $P_O(a,b\mid x,y)$ is normalized; $\mathcal C_O$ is the declared channel/interface carrier with wing map $\ell_O^{C}:\mathcal C_O\to\{A,B,AB\}$; $\alpha_O:\mathcal C_O\to\{0,1\}$ is its reference anchor-admission verdict; $\theta_O$ is the target identifier; $\mathsf Z_O$ is the finite set of evaluated witness tokens with reference standing valuation $\sigma_O:\mathsf Z_O\to\{0,1\}$ and wing label
\[
\ell_O^{Z}:\mathsf Z_O\to\{A,B,AB\};
\]
$\eta_O$ is the witness-act identifier; $\mathsf S_O$ is the reuse-state carrier with wing label $\ell_O^{S}:\mathsf S_O\to\{A,B,AB\}$ and reference continuation relation $\mathcal K_O\subseteq\mathsf S_O\times\mathsf S_O$; $\mathsf{Rpt}_O$ is the report-token carrier with wing label $\ell_O^{R}:\mathsf{Rpt}_O\to\{A,B,AB\}$, reference report/reuse verdict $\rho_O:\mathsf{Rpt}_O\sqcup\mathsf S_O\to\{0,1\}$, and support map $\zeta_O:\mathsf{Rpt}_O\sqcup\mathsf S_O\to\mathsf Z_O$; $\mathsf W_O$ is a finite family of functions from empirical tables to $\{-1,0,1\}$, with reference comparison profile $\omega_O(w)=w(P_O)$; and $\tau_O$ is the reference act-time status. Write $\mathcal C_O^A,\mathcal C_O^B,\mathcal C_O^{AB}$, $\mathsf Z_O^A,\mathsf Z_O^B,\mathsf Z_O^{AB}$, $\mathsf S_O^A,\mathsf S_O^B,\mathsf S_O^{AB}$, and $\mathsf{Rpt}_O^A,\mathsf{Rpt}_O^B,\mathsf{Rpt}_O^{AB}$ for the corresponding label fibers.

The displayed values determine the reference section formalized next. They specify the declared target packet used to type lawful transport; they are not a second candidate governance proposal. Candidate governance values are supplied only by the semantic answer section introduced below. No semantic claim that the six later governance loci are exhaustive is used here.

\]
The complete typed query carrier is the displayed disjoint union
$\mathsf Q_{\Bell}(O):=\bigsqcup_L\mathsf Q_L(O)$; it is a syntactic interface, not yet a semantic exhaustion theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Exactly recoverable operational simulation]\label{def:operationalsimulation}
An exactly recoverable simulation $f:O\to O'$ consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(S\arabic*),leftmargin=3em]
\item setting maps $f_X,f_Y$, contextwise stochastic kernels
\[
K^{A,f}_{x}:A^O_x\to\Delta(A^{O'}_{f_Xx}),\qquad
K^{B,f}_{y}:B^O_y\to\Delta(B^{O'}_{f_Yy}),
\]
and recovery data of the same type;
\item maps on channel, standing-token, reuse-state, report, and comparison carriers preserving the channel, token, reuse-state, and report wing labels, support maps, target identifiers, and act identifiers on the admitted witness;
\item the stochastic pushforward and recovery equations
\[
P_{O'}=f_*P_O,\qquad P_O=r_{f*}P_{O'},
\]
with both composites equal to the identity on the admitted witness table;
\item query reindexing maps $f_L^{\sharp}$ and $r_L^{\sharp}$ induced by the carrier maps for every non-cell query, together with dependent fiber maps
\[
\varphi^L_{f,q}:\mathsf V_L(O,q)\longrightarrow
\mathsf V_L(O',f_L^{\sharp}q)
\]
that are inverse to the corresponding recovery maps on admitted query fibers;
\item induced section transports $F_f^L$, not independent extra data: on empirical-cell coordinates $F_f^T$ is the stochastic pushforward by $K^{A,f}\otimes K^{B,f}$; on standing, comparison, anchor, continuation, act-time, and report/reuse coordinates it is dependent reindexing through $f_L^{\sharp}$ and $\varphi^L_{f,q}$; and local realization coordinates are the marginals of the transported joint realization support;
\item reference-section naturality:
\[
F_f^L\bigl(\RefSec_L(O)\bigr)=\RefSec_L(O')
\qquad(L\in\{D,A,T,C,\tau,U\}),
\]
where $\RefSec_L$ is the $L$-coordinate of \cref{def:earlyreferencesection}.
\end{enumerate}
The induced transports satisfy
\[
F_f^L:\mathscr X_L(O)\xrightarrow{\cong}\mathscr X_L(O'),\qquad
R_f^L:\mathscr X_L(O')\xrightarrow{\cong}\mathscr X_L(O),
\]
\[
R_f^LF_f^L=\mathrm{id},\qquad F_f^LR_f^L=\mathrm{id},
\]
and commute with the query and response maps. Globally section-isomorphic refinements, dilations, ancillas, coarse-grainings, convex re-expressions, or implementation changes enter the lawful class only when the complete section spaces are isomorphic, the reference section is natural, and the exact recovery equations are proved. Thus the lawful category contains globally section-isomorphic exactly recoverable simulations, not arbitrary operational coarse-grainings.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Base operational category]\label{def:baseoperationalcategory}
Let $\BellBase$ be the category whose objects are finite operational Bell objects and whose arrows are exactly recoverable operational simulations. Identity and composition are componentwise; effect transport satisfies
\[
F_{g\circ f}^L=F_g^L\circ F_f^L,
\qquad F_{\mathrm{id}}^L=\mathrm{id}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Canonical Bell transformation]\label{def:canonicalbelltransformation}
A canonical Bell transformation is an exactly recoverable simulation whose setting, outcome, channel, token, reuse, report, and comparison maps are bijections and whose transport maps are the induced reindexings. Its probability action is
\[
(g\!\cdot\!P)\bigl(\sigma^A_x(a),\sigma^B_y(b)\mid\sigma_X(x),\sigma_Y(y)\bigr)=P(a,b\mid x,y).
\]
Write $\BellCan$ for the resulting isomorphism groupoid inside $\BellBase$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical Bell transformations form a groupoid]\label{lem:canonicalgroupoid}
The identity belongs to $\BellCan$, canonical transformations compose, and each has a canonical inverse.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All carrier maps are bijections and all effect transports are reindexing isomorphisms. Identity, composition, and inverse are componentwise.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Canonical-invariance theorem]\label{thm:canonicalinvariance}
Canonical Bell transformations preserve normalization, overlap consistency, operational no-signaling, global-section status, comparison profile, anchor verdicts, act identity, continuation, and licensed report/reuse.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Bijective pushforward preserves total probability and commutes with marginalization. The remaining statements are invariance of the corresponding typed effect sections under the reindexing isomorphisms.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Lawful equivalence and its effect functors}

\begin{definition}[Lawful Bell zigzag and groupoid completion]\label{def:lawfulequivalence}
A lawful Bell zigzag from $O$ to $O'$ is a finite zigzag in $\BellBase$ whose legs are exactly recoverable simulations. Write $O\BellEq O'$ when such a zigzag exists. Let $\BellLaw$ be the groupoid completion generated by these arrows, modulo identities, composition, and cancellation with formal inverses.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Lawful Bell equivalence is an equivalence relation]\label{lem:lawfulequivalence}
The relation $\BellEq$ is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive, and $\BellCan$ embeds fully into $\BellLaw$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the empty zigzag, reversal, and concatenation. Canonical isomorphisms are generating exactly recoverable simulations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Object-indexed governance-effect functors]\label{thm:effectfunctors}
For each $L\in\{D,A,T,C,\tau,U\}$, the assignments
\[
O\longmapsto\mathscr X_L(O),\qquad
f\longmapsto F_f^L
\]
extend uniquely to a functor
\[
\mathscr X_L:\BellLaw\longrightarrow\mathbf{Set}_{\cong}.
\]
For a zigzag $h$, the transport $h_*^L$ is the corresponding composite of the $F$ maps and their inverses and is independent of the chosen representative of $h$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The functor laws hold on $\BellBase$ by definition. The universal property of groupoid completion extends every functor that sends generating arrows to isomorphisms. Zigzag relations map to identity, composition, and inverse equations, so the induced transport is representative-independent.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Diagonal effect transport]\label{def:diagonaleffecttransport}
For an arrow $h:O\to O'$ in $\BellLaw$ and a six-effect tuple $e=(e_D,e_A,e_T,e_C,e_{\tau},e_U)$ over $O$, define
\[
h_*e:=(h_*^De_D,h_*^Ae_A,h_*^Te_T,h_*^Ce_C,h_*^{\tau}e_{\tau},h_*^Ue_U).
\]
This is the diagonal action of the six functors of \cref{thm:effectfunctors}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lawful-localization closure]\label{thm:lawfulequivalencecomplete}
Every arrow of $\BellLaw$ preserves the typed operational carrier, the admitted empirical table, and the reference target packet of its source and target. It also transports arbitrary candidate effect sections through the six functors of \cref{thm:effectfunctors}; it does not identify a candidate section with the reference section. Consequently normalization, overlap consistency, and the reference target data are invariant under $\BellEq$, while candidate governance behavior is compared only after functorial transport. Global-section invariance is proved separately for the empirical lawful groupoid in \cref{thm:empiricalglobalinvariance}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each generator preserves the typed carrier and satisfies the coordinatewise reference-section naturality equation of \cref{def:operationalsimulation}; functorial transport carries arbitrary candidate sections under composition and formal inversion.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Raw typed semantic presentation]\label{def:bellgovpresentation}

\begin{aligned}

&\sum_b\beta_G^T(g)(q_{\rm cell}(x,y,a,b))
 =\sum_b\beta_G^T(g)(q_{\rm cell}(x,y',a,b)) &&\forall x,a,y,y',\\

 =\sum_a\beta_G^T(g)(q_{\rm cell}(x',y,a,b)) &&\forall y,b,x,x',\\

\begin{definition}[Target-active extensional governance equivalence]\label{def:governanceeffect}

\begin{theorem}[Extensional governance equivalence]\label{thm:governanceequivalence}
The relation $\GovEq$ is an equivalence relation on the presentations claiming the fixed target $P$.

\subsection{Physical occurrence and the neutral target}

\paragraph{Finite mechanized scaling.}
In the Lean companion, joint probabilities are integer weights over denominator $32$. The vector/Born witness has
\[
S_{32}=80,\qquad S=\frac{80}{32}=\frac52,
\]
while the Bell-local bound is $|S_{32}|\le64$. The exact positive-bound gap is $16/32=1/2$. This is a finite witness implementation, not a replacement for the general Bell domain.

\begin{definition}[Physical Bell correlation occurrence]
A physical Bell correlation occurrence is the occurrence of a Bell-type operational family, including preparation, settings, detector events, outcomes, records, and estimated probabilities. Physical occurrence alone does not fix non-degenerate same-witness use.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Ontic non-degenerate Bell-witness determinacy]\label{def:ndbell}
Let $g_P^*:=(d_P^*,\alpha_P^*,t_P^*,c_P^*,\tau_P^*,u_P^*)$ be the six reference effect sections induced by $O_P$. The predicate $\NDBell(P)$ holds iff
\[
\sigma_P|_{\mathsf Z_P^{\Active}}=\sigma_P^{\Active},
\]
the packet $\Theta_P$ is fixed under $\BellLaw$, the active anchor verdict is $\alpha_P^*|_{\mathcal C_P^{\Active}}$, the empirical-cell coordinates of $t_P^*$ are $P$, and
\[
\mathcal K_P\cap(\mathsf S_P^{\Active})^2=\mathcal K_P^{\Active},
\qquad
\FinalP(\tau_P^*),
\]
while every act in $\MandRpt_P\sqcup\MandReuse_P$ is licensed by $u_P^*$ and supported by positive standing. Because every endpoint of $\MandCont_P$ lies in $\MandReuse_P$, every state in the active continuation generator is among the licensed reuse states. These are the identity conditions of the already instantiated target practice. No locality, tensor, causal, or transport form is included in this definition.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Physical occurrence is not yet Bell-witness determinacy]
For a Bell operational family $P$,
\[
\PhysBell(P)\not\Rightarrow\NDBell(P).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Physical occurrence supplies the empirical events. It does not by itself supply the fixed lawful orbit, admitted anchor, positive standing token, continuation relation, act-time finality, or licensed report/reuse data required by \cref{def:ndbell}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Boundary traces for Bell witness channels}

\begin{definition}[Bell boundary trace]\label{def:belltrace}
A Bell boundary trace is a declared predicate
\[
\Trace_{Ch}:\mathcal C_P\to\{0,1\}
\]
on candidate setting/outcome interfaces, detector channels, calibration links, readout identifications, or coincidence rules. It is predeclared and non-generative in the formal sense $\PreTrace(\Trace_{Ch})$, bivalent, invariant under $\AutBell$, outcome-independent by its channel-status factorization, and wing-local in the typed sense that every automorphism preserves $\ell_P^C$ and the trace is constant on the resulting fixed-wing channel orbits. It fixes anchor admission only; it does not create the Bell correlations or determine the comparison result.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[No arbitrary Bell singleton trace]\label{thm:noarbitrarytrace}
A singleton-valued channel predicate fixes a witness role only when its source is independently declared in the Bell-domain architecture and satisfies the trace conditions of \cref{def:belltrace}. Otherwise it is post-selection, hidden selector import, delayed repair, or scope change.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Singleton cardinality alone is trivial to manufacture after the data are known. Prior declaration blocks after-the-fact selection; canonical stability fixes the same interface under lawful re-expression; outcome independence blocks desired-result selection; wing locality blocks remote channel choice; and non-generativity blocks the predicate from creating its own standing.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Boundary-trace representation theorem]\label{thm:tracefactorization}
Let $I$ be a richer Bell-interface structure with an anchor-status map $s_I:I\to\{0,1\}$ satisfying prior declaration, bivalence, lawful transport invariance, outcome independence, wing locality, and non-generativity. Let $I/\!\sim_I$ be the quotient by the lawful channel-isomorphism relation induced by $\BellLaw$. Then there is a unique trace
\[
\overline{\Trace}_I:I/\!\sim_I\to\{0,1\}
\]
such that $s_I=\overline{\Trace}_I\circ q_I$. Every anchor-level standing verdict factors through this trace. The exclusion of a second anchor authority is supplied separately by same-scope closure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lawful transport invariance makes $s_I$ constant on $\sim_I$-classes, so the quotient universal property supplies the unique trace. The remaining trace conditions prevent result-conditioned or self-generating anchor status. Boundary-trace fixation and same-scope closure then exclude an independent second anchor authority \cite{MaleyBoundaryTrace2026,MaleyBivalence2026}.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Kernel-licensed same-domain Bell act]\label{def:samedomainact}

\item $\neg\operatorname{FreshGate}(\Phi)$;
\item the status pair belongs to $\mathsf{SA}_P$;
\item the lawful carrier arrow preserves the declared act interface, wing maps, support maps, and pre-act trace register; and
\item no fresh target token, gate token, channel-status rule, or act identifier occurs in the target section.

\begin{definition}[Standing-flow effects]\label{def:standingflow}
For a same-domain modification $\Phi:G\to G'$ with lawful transport $h$, define:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.0em,style=nextline]
\item[$\CreatesStand(\Phi)$]
$\neg\operatorname{FreshGate}(\Phi)$ and there is $z$ with
\[
\operatorname{st}_{h_*^TE_T(G)}(z)=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{st}_{E_T(G')}(z)=1.
\]
\item[$\AmplifiesStand(\Phi)$]
$\neg\operatorname{FreshGate}(\Phi)$ and $h_*^TE_T(G)\prec_P E_T(G')$.
\item[$\TransfersStand(\Phi)$]
Either $\Phi$ is $A$-supported and at least one of the following changes: standing on $\mathsf Z_P^B\cup\mathsf Z_P^{AB}$; a $B$-local or joint realization coordinate $q^B_{\rm real}(y,b)$ or $q^{AB}_{\rm real}(x,y,a,b)$; a continuation coordinate $q_{\rm cont}(s,s')$ with $s$ or $s'$ in $\mathsf S_P^B\cup\mathsf S_P^{AB}$; or report/reuse behavior on $\mathsf{Rpt}_P^B\cup\mathsf{Rpt}_P^{AB}\cup\mathsf S_P^B\cup\mathsf S_P^{AB}$. The $B$-supported case is symmetric with $A$ and $B$ exchanged. Joint $AB$ coordinates count as distant governance whenever a one-wing act changes them.
\item[$\RepairsAct(\Phi)$]
The domain and act identifier are fixed, the act-time status changes, and there is $z$ with
\[
\operatorname{st}_{h_*^TE_T(G)}(z)=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{st}_{E_T(G')}(z)=1.
\]
\item[$\MetricAuth(\Phi)$]
The transported target, active trace data, empirical cells, comparison profile, act identifier, wing maps, and support maps agree, while
\[
m_G(g_0)\neq m_{G'}(g'_0)
\]
and at least one target-bearing standing, continuation, report/reuse, or act-time verdict differs. Thus metric authority means that metric metadata is the only new discriminator of a governance verdict; it is not defined by an anchor-bit change and is not excluded merely by $\neg\operatorname{FreshGate}(\Phi)$.
\end{description}
These are explicit relations on transported, wing-indexed effect sections.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Primitive same-domain standing-flow exclusion]\label{thm:primitivenotransfer}
For every kernel-licensed same-domain Bell act $\Phi$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\neg\CreatesStand(\Phi)\wedge\neg\AmplifiesStand(\Phi)
 \wedge\neg\TransfersStand(\Phi)\\
&\qquad\wedge\neg\RepairsAct(\Phi)\wedge\neg\MetricAuth(\Phi).
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a kernel-licensed same-act transformation, standing conservation excludes creation, amplification, transfer, and same-act restoration \cite[Theorem~6.15]{MaleyKernel2026}; the Bivalence Theorem gives the same-scope form \cite[Theorem~3.6]{MaleyBivalence2026}. For the metric-authority branch, the declared nonmetric target packet and trace data are fixed while a target-bearing verdict changes. By \cref{thm:semanticfactorization}, that change must occupy one of the canonical governance-effect channels. Because metric metadata is neither part of the declared gate nor a reference-bearing target coordinate, governance-bearing dependence on it is an independent same-domain discriminator or second invariant; AMetric no-selector closure and no-second-invariant rigidity exclude that route \cite[Theorem~2.5]{MaleyAMetricBoundary2026}\cite[Theorem~3.4]{MaleyBivalence2026}. If the metadata changes no target-bearing effect, it is skin. Same-scope operator closure independently excludes post-hoc repair, deferred admissibility, and any attempted metric selection operator \cite[Corollaries~3.11--3.12 and Proposition~5.2]{MaleySameScope2026}. The displayed predicates are exactly the Bell effect-section substitutions of those prohibited routes.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Empirical model and standing quotient}

\begin{definition}[Bell measurement cover and empirical model]\label{def:empiricalmodel}
Let the measurement labels be $\{A_x:x\in X\}\cup\{B_y:y\in Y\}$ and the contexts be
\[
\mathcal M_{\Bell}=\{C_{xy}=\{A_x,B_y\}:x\in X,\ y\in Y\}.
\]
The empirical model determined by $P$ is
\[
e_P=\{P_{xy}\}_{(x,y)},\qquad P_{xy}(a,b)=P(a,b\mid x,y).
\]
It is compatible on overlaps iff context distributions induce the same one-wing marginal whenever contexts share a local measurement. In a Bell scenario this is operational no-signaling. Let $\BellEmp$ denote the compatible empirical models on this fixed cover and outcome typing.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Exactly recoverable empirical simulation]\label{def:empiricalsimulation}
Let $e$ and $e'$ be finite compatible empirical models, possibly on lawfully equivalent finite covers. An exactly recoverable empirical simulation consists of setting maps, contextwise outcome kernels and recovery kernels, together with mutually inverse affine maps
\[
\widehat f:\Delta(\Omega_e)\xrightarrow{\cong}\Delta(\Omega_{e'}),
\qquad
\widehat r:\Delta(\Omega_{e'})\xrightarrow{\cong}\Delta(\Omega_e),
\]
where $\Omega_e=\prod_xA_x\times\prod_yB_y$, such that
\[
e'=f_*e,\qquad e=r_*e',
\]
and for every global distribution $\nu$ and context $(x,y)$,
\[
\operatorname{marg}_{f(x),f(y)}(\widehat f\nu)
=f_*\operatorname{marg}_{x,y}(\nu),
\]
with the analogous recovery equation. Thus global assignments and their context marginals are transported by the same empirical simulation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Empirical lawful groupoid and equivalence]\label{def:empiricallawfulgroupoid}
Let $\EmpLaw$ be the groupoid completion of the category of finite compatible empirical models and exactly recoverable empirical simulations. Write
\[
e\EmpEq e'
\]
iff there is an arrow from $e$ to $e'$ in $\EmpLaw$. This relation is defined directly on empirical models and is independent of any governance packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Empirical lawful equivalence and global-section invariance]\label{thm:empiricalglobalinvariance}
The relation $\EmpEq$ is an equivalence relation. Every arrow $g:e\to e'$ in $\EmpLaw$ induces a bijection
\[
\widehat g:\GlobalSec(e)\xrightarrow{\cong}\GlobalSec(e').
\]
Consequently Bell-locality and Bell nonlocality are invariant under $\EmpEq$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity follow from the groupoid completion. If $\nu\in\GlobalSec(e)$, the commuting marginal equations give
$\operatorname{marg}(\widehat g\nu)=g_*e=e'$, so $\widehat g\nu\in\GlobalSec(e')$. The inverse arrow supplies the inverse map.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Lawful Bell presentation class and transported active profile]\label{def:bellprofile}
Let $\mathcal P_{\Bell}(P)$ be the class of presentations $G\in\BellGov$ whose operational object lies in $[O_P]_{\BellEq}$ and whose transported target identifier and act identifier are $\theta_P$ and $\eta_P$. For $G\in\mathcal P_{\Bell}(P)$ choose any lawful arrow $h_G:O_G\to O_P$ and define
\[
\BellProf^{P}(G):=
\Bigl[\ActRed\bigl(h_{G*}\GovEff(G)\bigr)\Bigr]_{\AutBell}.
\]
If $h_G'$ is another choice, then $h_G'h_G^{-1}\in\AutBell$, so the orbit is choice-independent. The profile contains exactly the active anchor, empirical/comparison/standing, realization/continuation, final-status, and mandatory report/reuse behavior. Inactive coordinates are absent and cannot obstruct collapse of governance-equivalent presentations.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bell-profile completeness]\label{thm:bellprofilecomplete}
Assume $\NDBell(P)$. Every same-domain target-bearing Bell verdict on a presentation in $\mathcal P_{\Bell}(P)$ is determined by $\BellProf^{P}$. If two lawful presentations have equal transported active profiles, every remaining difference is governance-inert skin; if a difference changes the target practice, it changes at least one active reduced coordinate or leaves the fixed domain.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Kernel-activated semantic exhaustiveness is \cref{thm:semanticfactorization}. The diagonal profile contains the active reductions of all six effect channels in one common fiber. The imported null-effect theorem routes differences outside the active target-bearing fragment to skin.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Bell standing equivalence]\label{def:bellequiv}

\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[Exact Bell standing quotient]\label{thm:bellquotient}

The substantive result is profile completeness in \cref{thm:bellprofilecomplete}; the present statement is the quotient universal-property corollary.
\end{corollary}


\subsection{Bell-local completion and global-section obstruction}

\begin{definition}[Global-section set]\label{def:globalsectionset}
For a finite empirical model $e_P$, define
\[
\GlobalSec(e_P)=\left\{\nu\in\Delta\!\left(\prod_{x\in X}A_x\times\prod_{y\in Y}B_y\right):
\operatorname{marg}_{x,y}(\nu)=P_{xy}\ \text{for every }(x,y)\right\}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Marginal fiber, Bell-local subfiber, and comparison profile]\label{def:marginalfiber}
Let
\[
\Marg(e)=\bigl(\{e|_{A_x}\}_{x\in X},\{e|_{B_y}\}_{y\in Y}\bigr),
\]
and define
\[
\BellFiber(P)=\{e\in\BellEmp:\Marg(e)=\Marg(e_P)\},
\qquad
\BellLocal(P)=\{e\in\BellFiber(P):\GlobalSec(e)\neq\varnothing\}.
\]
Let $\BellWit(P)$ be the declared Bell-comparison profile---the CHSH functional and relabeling-complete family in the binary case.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Independently sufficient local standing]\label{def:independentlocalstanding}
A Bell witness has independently sufficient local standing iff there exist a probability space $(\Lambda,\Sigma,\mu)$, with $\mu$ independent of the settings, and normalized measurable stochastic kernels
\[
P_A(a\mid x,\lambda),\qquad P_B(b\mid y,\lambda),
\]
such that local responses exhaust the standing-bearing contribution and
\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)=\int_{\Lambda}P_A(a\mid x,\lambda)P_B(b\mid y,\lambda)\,d\mu(\lambda).
\]
This is the AASC structural counterpart of Bell-local factorization.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Local standing, Bell-locality, and global completion]\label{thm:localstandingfactorization}
For a finite Bell scenario, independently sufficient local standing yields Bell-local factorization and a global section. Conversely, every global section is a convex mixture of deterministic global assignments and supplies a Bell-local model. In the binary CHSH scenario, these conditions are equivalent to the complete CHSH inequality family \cite{Fine1982,AbramskyBrandenburger2011}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each $\lambda$, define
\[
\nu_{\lambda}(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=
\prod_xP_A(a_x\mid x,\lambda)\prod_yP_B(b_y\mid y,\lambda),
\]
and integrate over $\mu$. Marginalizing to $(x,y)$ recovers the Bell-local factorization. Conversely, use a global assignment as the hidden variable, with deterministic local responses given by its coordinates. Fine's theorem supplies the binary CHSH equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Bell nonlocality is global-section obstruction]\label{cor:localstandingexcluded}
If the admitted witness violates the relevant Bell inequality, then $\GlobalSec(e_P)=\varnothing$ and independently sufficient local standing is unavailable.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Bell joint obstruction packet}

\begin{definition}[Bell obstruction diagram and packet]\label{def:jointinvariant}
Define the pointed Bell obstruction diagram
\[
\mathbf J(P):=\bigl(e_P,\Marg(e_P),\BellFiber(P),\BellLocal(P),
\GlobalSec(e_P)\bigr),
\]
with the evident marginal, inclusion, and global-section maps. Every arrow $g:e\to e'$ in $\EmpLaw$ induces affine bijections

g_*^{\rm fib}:\BellFiber(e)\xrightarrow{\cong}\BellFiber(e'),
\qquad
g_*^{\rm loc}:\BellLocal(e)\xrightarrow{\cong}\BellLocal(e'),
\]
together with the global-section bijection of \cref{thm:empiricalglobalinvariance}. The comparison profile is added only at the governance and compatibility layer, because it is not a field of the empirical simulation category. Let

\]
be the isomorphism class of this pointed diagram. For Bell-nonlocal $P$,

\]
The packet is functorial up to empirical lawful isomorphism. Its asserted irreducibility is governance-role irreducibility, not coordinate minimality.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reduced Bell obstruction equivalence]\label{def:reducedobstruction}
On $\BellFiber(P)$ let $R_{\mathrm{loc}}$ be the relation
\[
e\,R_{\mathrm{loc}}\,e'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(e\EmpEq e')\ \lor\ (e,e'\in\BellLocal(P)).
\]
Let $\approx_{\mathrm{loc}}$ be the smallest equivalence relation containing $R_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Equivalently, it is the equivalence closure of lawful Bell equivalence together with the complete relation on the Bell-local subfiber. Define
\[
\ObBell(P):=[e_P]_{\approx_{\mathrm{loc}}}\in\BellFiber(P)/\!\approx_{\mathrm{loc}}.
\]
The common class of $\BellLocal(P)$ is the distinguished base point.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Local base point is separated from Bell-nonlocal classes]\label{lem:obstructionseparation}
Empirical lawful equivalence preserves existence of a global section. Hence no Bell-nonlocal empirical model is $\approx_{\mathrm{loc}}$-equivalent to the Bell-local base point.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Global-section existence is invariant under $\EmpEq$ by \cref{thm:empiricalglobalinvariance}. Every generating pair added beyond lawful equivalence lies entirely inside $\BellLocal(P)$. A finite chain beginning in the Bell-local subfiber therefore cannot leave it.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Functorial canonicality and role nontriviality]\label{thm:jointinvariantcanonical}
The assignment $e\mapsto\mathbf J(e)$ is functorial on $\EmpLaw$ and sends every arrow to an isomorphism of pointed obstruction diagrams. Hence $\JOb(P)$ is invariant as an isomorphism class. The reduced class $\ObBell(P)$ is invariant under the induced fiber isomorphisms. If $P$ is Bell-nonlocal, $\ObBell(P)$ is not the base point, and no independently sufficient local-standing model has the same packet or reduced obstruction class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The affine global-assignment bijection commutes with all context marginals, hence transports the marginal fiber. Global-section invariance transports the Bell-local subfiber and the distinguished base point. Identity and composition follow from the empirical functor laws. Bell nonlocality gives $e_P\notin\BellLocal(P)$, so \cref{lem:obstructionseparation} makes its class non-base-point. Independently sufficient local standing supplies a global section by \cref{thm:localstandingfactorization} and therefore lies at the base point.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Raw compatibility data, certification, and exact equivalence}

\begin{definition}[Raw Bell compatibility data]\label{def:tensorcompat}
A raw Bell compatibility datum for the fixed witness $P$ is

where:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(R\arabic*),leftmargin=3em]
\item $\nu^T_{xy}:A_x\times B_y\to[0,1]$ is a context distribution;
\item $\sigma_T:\mathsf Z_P\to\{0,1\}$ is the standing-token valuation;
\item $r^{A,T}_{xy},r^{B,T}_{xy}$ are the marginal maps;
\item $\mathcal R_T^A\subseteq\bigsqcup_x\{x\}\times A_x$, $\mathcal R_T^B\subseteq\bigsqcup_y\{y\}\times B_y$, and $\mathcal R_T^{AB}\subseteq\bigsqcup_{x,y}\{(x,y)\}\times A_x\times B_y$ are the wing-local and joint realization supports;

\end{enumerate}
The domain orbit, anchor effect, and act-time status are fixed externally by $P$ and are not duplicated inside the compatibility datum.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compatibility certificate predicate]\label{def:compatcertificate}
For $T^{\mathrm{raw}}\in\BellCompatRaw$, define
\[
\CompatCert(T^{\mathrm{raw}})
\]
to hold iff:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*),leftmargin=3em]
\item every $\nu^T_{xy}$ is normalized;
\item the marginal maps agree on overlaps;
\item $\sigma_T=\sigma_P$;
\item $\mathcal R_T^{AB}=\bigsqcup_{x,y}\{(x,y)\}\times\operatorname{supp}\nu^T_{xy}$ and $\mathcal R_T^A,\mathcal R_T^B$ are its one-wing marginal supports;
\item $\mathcal K_T$ is a relation on $\mathsf S_P^{\Active}$ with $\mathcal K_T\ContEq\mathcal K_P^{\Active}$; target and act identity are fixed by the ambient $O_P$ fiber rather than reasserted as an untyped property of the relation;
\item $\operatorname{cov}_T$ satisfies the identity and composition laws for $\AutBell$ under $\mathscr X_T$ and $\mathscr X_C$;
\item $\omega_T$ is the comparison profile computed from $\nu^T$ on the declared test family; and
\item for every $q\in\mathsf Q_{U,P}^{\Active}$, a positive report or reuse verdict satisfies $\sigma_T(\bar\zeta_P(q))=1$ and $\ContUse(\mathcal K_T;q)=1$. No certificate condition is imposed on inactive report/reuse coordinates; they are skin unless they alter an active consequence.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Certified Bell compatibility structure]\label{def:certifiedcompatibility}

Proof terms are not governance-bearing data; only the extensional fields they certify enter equivalence.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Type-level compatibility role separation]\label{prop:compattyperoleseparation}
A raw compatibility datum has no domain-effect, anchor-effect, metric-authority, or act-time field. Hence any such authority attributed to $T$ is not encoded by the compatibility type and must enter through an external governance coordinate. Under kernel-activated effect-channel uniqueness, it is either the fixed target value, skin, a second classifier, repair, or scope change.
\end{proposition}

\begin{definition}[Separated compatibility effects]\label{def:standingeffectmap}
For $T\in\BellCompat$, define three effect sections:

Anchor, domain, and act-time information is deliberately absent from $\StandEff$ and supplied separately by the fixed target.

For $T\in\BellCompat$, define $\ExactFaithful(T)$ to hold iff the following target-bearing conditions hold after lawful transport:

\item the marginal maps are $r^{A,P}_{xy},r^{B,P}_{xy}$;

\item $\mathcal R_T^{AB}=\Real(P)$, $\mathcal R_T^A=\Real^A(P)$, and $\mathcal R_T^B=\Real^B(P)$;

\item $\omega_T|_{\mathsf W_P^{\Active}}=\omega_P|_{\mathsf W_P^{\Active}}$;
\item $\ReuseEff(T)$ agrees with the target report/reuse effect on $\mathsf Q_{U,P}^{\Active}$; and
\item $\GlobalSec(e_P)=\varnothing$.
\end{enumerate}

\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Semantic target deviations and terminal discharge predicates]\label{def:jointdischarge}
For $G\in\BellGov$, let $\operatorname{emp}(E_T(G))$ be the empirical model given by the cell coordinates of $E_T(G)$. Define:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.2em,style=nextline]
\item[$\operatorname{AnchorDeviation}(G)$]
$\SemClaims(G,P)$ and $\operatorname{red}_{P,A}(E_A(G))\neq\alpha_P^*|_{\mathcal C_P^{\Active}}$ after lawful transport.
\item[$\operatorname{ContinuationDeviation}(G)$]
$\SemClaims(G,P)$ and $\operatorname{red}_{P,C}(E_C(G))\neq\operatorname{red}_{P,C}(\ContEff(T_B(P)))$.
\item[$\operatorname{ActTimeDeviation}(G)$]
$\SemClaims(G,P)$ and $\operatorname{red}_{P,\tau}(E_{\tau}(G))\neq f_P$.
\item[$\operatorname{ReuseDeviation}(G)$]
$\SemClaims(G,P)$ and $\operatorname{red}_{P,U}(E_U(G))\neq\operatorname{red}_{P,U}(\ReuseEff(T_B(P)))$.
\item[$\operatorname{EmpiricalDeviation}(G)$]
$\SemClaims(G,P)$ and the transported empirical cells are not equal to $e_P$ in the fixed $O_P$ fiber.
\item[$\operatorname{ComparisonDeviation}(G)$]
The empirical cells represent $e_P$ but $\operatorname{cmp}_{E_T(G)}|_{\mathsf W_P^{\Active}}\neq\omega_P|_{\mathsf W_P^{\Active}}$.
\item[$\operatorname{StandingTokenDeviation}(G)$]
The empirical and active comparison coordinates agree with $e_P$ and $\omega_P$, but $\operatorname{st}_{E_T(G)}|_{\mathsf Z_P^{\Active}}\neq\sigma_P|_{\mathsf Z_P^{\Active}}$.
\item[$\operatorname{BellLocalCompletion}(G)$]
$\GlobalSec(\operatorname{emp}(E_T(G)))\neq\varnothing$.
\item[$\operatorname{StandingFlow}(G)$]
There are $G_0$ and a kernel-licensed $\Phi:G_0\to G$ with $\SemClaims(G_0,P)$ such that one of
\[
\CreatesStand(\Phi),\quad \AmplifiesStand(\Phi),\quad
\TransfersStand(\Phi),\quad \MetricAuth(\Phi)
\]
holds.
\item[$\operatorname{SameActRepair}(G)$]
There are $G_0$ and a kernel-licensed $\Phi:G_0\to G$ such that $\SemClaims(G_0,P)$ and $\RepairsAct(\Phi)$.
\item[$\operatorname{ScopeChange}(G)$]
There is no $h:O_G\to O_P$ in $\BellLaw$ carrying the target identifier, cover, channels, and act identifier to those of $P$.
\end{description}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Active-coordinate exhaustion]\label{thm:roleirreducibility}
Let $G\in\BellGov$ satisfy $\SemClaims(G,P)$. If its target-active profile differs from the canonical active profile, then at least one of the following holds:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\operatorname{AnchorDeviation}(G),\quad
\operatorname{ContinuationDeviation}(G),\\
\operatorname{ActTimeDeviation}(G),\quad
\operatorname{ReuseDeviation}(G),\\
\operatorname{EmpiricalDeviation}(G),\quad
\operatorname{ComparisonDeviation}(G),\\
\operatorname{StandingTokenDeviation}(G),\quad
\operatorname{BellLocalCompletion}(G),\\
\operatorname{StandingFlow}(G),\quad
\operatorname{SameActRepair}(G),\\
\operatorname{ScopeChange}(G).
\end{gathered}
\]
This theorem is a typed coordinate exhaustion only; it does not use later componentwise uniqueness to close continuation, temporal, or reuse deviations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Transport to the $O_P$ fiber and compare the six active reductions. A domain, anchor, continuation, status, or use difference gives the corresponding first-line deviation. If those agree, the active standing effect has exactly three blocks: empirical cells, comparison values, and active standing tokens. A difference gives empirical, comparison, or standing-token deviation. Bell-local empirical replacement is the distinguished global-completion subcase. A difference produced by a same-domain modification is additionally classified as standing flow or repair. These cases exhaust inequality of the reduced tuple.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Joint standing class]
A joint standing class is a Bell standing class whose exact standing object satisfies the compatibility certificate. It is Bell-nonlocal when $\GlobalSec(e_P)=\varnothing$ and quantum-entangled when it additionally has an entangled realization in the carrier of \cref{def:quantumrealization}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Joint standing class as the Bell-nonlocal standing form]\label{prop:jscnormal}
A compatible Bell-nonlocal empirical model used as one non-degenerate witness has the same-domain standing form of a joint standing class carrying $T_B(P)$. It is not representable on that domain as independently sufficient local standing plus a later connector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The standing quotient fixes the witness profile, \cref{thm:residualtensor} certifies $T_B(P)$, and \cref{thm:compatibilityuniqueness} gives exact same-domain uniqueness. Independently sufficient local standing supplies a global section. A later connector instantiates standing transfer, a second invariant, or domain change.
\end{proof}


\paragraph{Relation to established frameworks.}
The global-section construction makes the relation to Fine and Abramsky--Brandenburger explicit: $\JOb(P)$ carries the same empirical model and obstruction to Bell-local global completion, while AASC adds fixed-domain anchor, act-time, and reuse roles. Jarrett--Shimony locality distinctions concern factorization inside candidate causal or ontological models; the present theorem classifies the reusable witness and does not infer the absence of every richer-scope nonlocal model. Spekkens-style operational equivalence and extensional governance equivalence are neighboring but distinct structures \cite{Jarrett1984,Shimony1986,Spekkens2005}.

\section{AMetric co-standing and exact local realization}\label{sec:costanding}

Quantum theory or another operational carrier supplies the numerical context distributions. AASC fixes the admissible same-domain type of the object carrying them. The Bell wings are local roles of one compatible empirical model, not independently complete standing states that acquire the fixed witness by later communication.

\begin{definition}[Local Bell face]\label{def:localbellface}
For $T_B(P)$ and setting $x$, the $A$-local face is the restriction of $\mu^P_{xy}$ to $A_x$ through its declared channel; the result is independent of $y$ by overlap consistency. The $B$-local face is defined analogously. A local face is a restriction of the joint empirical model, not an independently sufficient local standing state.

\begin{theorem}[Operational no-signaling forbids a controllable communication channel]\label{thm:ns1ns2}

\begin{definition}[Finite-dimensional quantum realization]\label{def:quantumrealization}
A finite-dimensional quantum realization of $P$ in the specified bipartite tensor-product and local-POVM carrier is a density operator $\rho_{AB}$ on finite-dimensional $\HS_A\otimes\HS_B$ and local POVMs $\{M^A_{a\mid x}\}$ and $\{M^B_{b\mid y}\}$ such that
\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\rho_{AB}\bigl(M^A_{a\mid x}\otimes M^B_{b\mid y}\bigr)\right].
\]
The realization is separable iff
\[
\rho_{AB}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}q_k\rho^A_k\otimes\rho^B_k,
\qquad q_k\ge0,\quad\sum_kq_k=1.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-dimensional separable realizations are Bell-local]\label{thm:separablebelllocal}
If a finite-dimensional realization of $P$ is separable, then every operational family generated by its local POVMs has a Bell-local factorization and a global section.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

\[
P(a,b\mid x,y)=\sum_k q_k

\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^B_kM^B_{b\mid y}),
\]

\end{proof}
