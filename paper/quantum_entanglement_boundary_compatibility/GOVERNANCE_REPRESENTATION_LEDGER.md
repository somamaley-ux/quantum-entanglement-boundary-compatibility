## Act support

`SameActSupport` is the equality predicate for endpoint act-support labels. `ActSupp` is the typed support value in `{A,B,AB}`. A one-wing modification must satisfy both the equality predicate and the corresponding typed support value.

## Compatibility continuation

The canonical compatibility object carries `K_P^act`, not the unrestricted reference relation. This aligns the compatibility type with target-bearing continuation work and makes certificate C5 direct.

## Retyping and metric authority

Preserving the reference-bearing target packet leaves a same-target governance proposal for classification; it does not by itself make the proposal skin. Metric-dependent governance differences are routed through semantic effect exhaustion and excluded as second same-domain invariants or selectors unless they are governance-inert skin.

## Universality boundary
